About FastImgConvert

FastImgConvert is a small browser-based utility for converting common image files to JPG, PNG or WebP. The goal is to make routine format conversion straightforward without requiring an account or a separate desktop application.

How the tool works

When you select an image, the converter loads it in your browser and uses browser image and canvas capabilities to create the requested output. The converter does not intentionally send selected image contents to an image-processing server.

Limitations

Browser support determines which source images can be decoded successfully. Animated images are not preserved as animations by a canvas-based conversion. Converting to JPEG removes transparency, and converting an already compressed image cannot restore lost detail.

Educational content

FastImgConvert also publishes concise guides explaining image formats and compression so users can choose settings based on their needs rather than simply selecting the smallest file.
