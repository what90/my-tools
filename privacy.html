Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

FastImgConvert is designed so that image conversion is performed in the user's browser. The image-processing code does not send selected file contents to an image-processing server. Choosing the public sample downloads a bundled example image from this website.

Third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as Google AdSense and related Google technologies. Those services may use cookies, device information or similar technologies for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation, subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Server and hosting data

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL and timestamps for security, delivery and operational purposes.

Files selected in the converter

The conversion feature processes selected images locally using browser APIs. Download links created by the converter use temporary browser object URLs for the current session.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's functionality or third-party services change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Advertising information

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on earlier visits to this or other websites. Google advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites. You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and read how Google uses information from partner sites.

Browser storage

The tool creates temporary object URLs for previews and downloads. It releases these when you clear the selection, start another conversion, or leave the page. The tool does not store selected image files in localStorage or IndexedDB.
